\section*{Limitations}
The voting threshold $\theta$ is swept on the same validation split it is
evaluated on, so per-relation optima are optimistic and not held-out; we report
no test numbers yet. Several margins are within plausible decoding noise:
\textsc{awardWonBy} has only 10 subjects, and we did not measure
run-to-run variance of the stochastic decoding ($N{=}3$, $n{=}5$), so
sub-$0.03$ deltas (e.g.\ the \textsc{personHasCityOfDeath} $+0.03$) are
indicative only. Being closed-book, the system inherits the base model's
coverage and recency limits and cannot recover facts it never stored, which caps
\textsc{awardWonBy} recall and rare-venue numeric accuracy. Because API rate
limits precluded multi-sample decoding for Gemma-4-31B, its scores are
single-sample; pairing the 30B with the self-consistency and abstention voting
that help the smaller models is left to future work and may lift the null-heavy
and numeric relations further. Finally, the
abstention advantage is a property of this task's macro metric and the
empty-as-valid-answer setting, and may not transfer to single-answer or
micro-only settings.
