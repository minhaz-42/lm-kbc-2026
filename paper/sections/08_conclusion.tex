\section{Conclusion}
We treated \emph{when to answer} as the central decision in closed-book,
set-valued KB construction: because macro-F\textsubscript{1} rewards a correct empty
set, abstention pays off, numeric recall is scarce, and scope grounding curbs over-
and under-prediction. With Gemma-4-31B it reaches $0.461$/$0.488$
(relation-averaged/micro), beating the official baseline on five of six relations,
reproducibly from a cached pass. Comparatively, a single-sample 30B outscores a
voted 14B, so pairing the two is the clearest next step.
