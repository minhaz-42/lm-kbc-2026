\section{Conclusion}
Every component of our system follows from one metric analysis: abstention pays off,
numeric recall is scarce, and scope grounding curbs over- and under-prediction.
With Gemma-4-31B it reaches $0.459$ relation-averaged macro-F\textsubscript{1}
($0.490$ micro), beating the official baseline on all six relations, and is fully
reproducible from a cached generation pass. A smaller Qwen2.5-14B, run locally in
4-bit, reaches $0.416$. That the 30B wins with single-sample decoding, while abstention and
self-consistency chiefly help the smaller models, suggests that model scale and
decode-time calibration are complementary levers whose balance shifts as models
get stronger.
