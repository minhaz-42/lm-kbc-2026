\section{Conclusion}
Every component of our system follows from a single metric analysis: abstention is valuable,
numeric recall is scarce, and scope grounding curbs over- and under-prediction.
With Gemma-4-31B it reaches $0.459$ relation-averaged macro-F\textsubscript{1}
($0.490$ micro), beating the official baseline on all six relations (0.416 with a
local 4-bit Qwen2.5-14B), and is fully reproducible from a cached generation
pass. That the 30B wins with single-sample decoding, while abstention and
self-consistency chiefly help the smaller models, suggests that model scale and
decode-time calibration are complementary levers whose balance shifts with model
strength.
