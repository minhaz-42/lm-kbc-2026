\section{Background and Related Work}
\paragraph{LMs as knowledge bases.} \citet{petroni2019lama} cast factual recall
as cloze probing; follow-ups sharpened the prompts \citep{jiang2020lpaqa,
shin2020autoprompt,zhong2021optiprompt}, quantified how much knowledge a model's
parameters hold \citep{roberts2020closedbook}, and questioned how reliably it can
be read out \citep{cao2021knowledgeable,alkhamissi2022review}. These probes
assume a single gold object and reward ranking it highly. We instead target the
set-valued, alias-graded variant in which the empty set is a valid answer---a
setting closer to materialising an actual KB \citep{pan2023llmkg}.

\paragraph{The LM-KBC shared task.} Across its editions
\citep{alivanistos2022prompting,kalo2023lmkbc,kalo2024lmkbc,kalo2025lmkbc} the
task has moved from masked-LM probing and prompt ensembling toward
instruction-tuned generative systems, with some earlier tracks permitting
retrieval or fine-tuning \citep{zhang2023llmke}. The 2026 edition's closed-book,
no-tuning, $\le$32B rules remove those options---which is precisely what makes our
parametric-recall-with-abstention design necessary.

\paragraph{Retrieval vs.\ closed book.} The usual route to knowledge-intensive
accuracy is retrieval augmentation \citep{lewis2020rag,guu2020realm}, which helps
most exactly when parametric memory is weak \citep{mallen2023whennot}. Self-RAG
\citep{asai2024selfrag} even lets the model decide \emph{when} to retrieve; we
borrow the spirit of a model-driven, adaptive decision but, since retrieval is
forbidden, redirect it to a different question---\emph{when to answer at all},
rather than what to fetch.

\paragraph{Self-consistency, calibration, and abstention.} These three threads
converge in our method. Self-consistency \citep{wang2022selfconsistency,chen2023universalsc}
samples a model repeatedly and aggregates the generations to pick a single
answer. Abstaining when uncertain is the classic reject option
\citep{geifman2017selective}, studied in NLP as calibration
\citep{guo2017calibration}, unanswerable QA \citep{rajpurkar2018squad2}, and
selective QA \citep{cole2023selective}, and LMs retain some awareness of what
they know \citep{kadavath2022know}. We combine the two: cross-sample
\emph{disagreement} \citep{kuhn2023semantic,manakul2023selfcheckgpt} is our
zero-parameter confidence signal, and---unlike majority-vote self-consistency,
which always emits an answer---we let it trigger \emph{abstention}, because here
the empty set is itself a valid, high-value prediction.
